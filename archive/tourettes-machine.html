About:
Tourettes Machine is a firefox plugin that randomly adds swear words to your
form fields and text areas as you type.

Versions:

Open source:

Rename the resulting youraddon.xpi.zip file by removing the .zip on the end and then drag the youraddon.xpi into a firefox window to install.
