We are well educated in the subtleties of academic writing

Academic writing has its features that differentiate it from, say, a newspaper article or a blog post. Firstly, it is characterized by complexity - but this complexity doesn't mean using sophisticated words instead of plain ones. In this type of writing, you have to make every word and statement count and avoid watery, fluffy sentences. It also assumes the use of terms and concepts from the specific field of study. Secondly, it's formal, so you have to omit conversational words, slang, or internet abbreviations. Thirdly, academic papers are objective, which means they are focused on the research, facts, and outcomes rather than on you as a writer and your personal impressions. And finally, they have to be precise and avoid vague statements like "Many people commute by car", using specific statistics and facts instead.

All these peculiarities make assignment writing a time consuming and demanding process. One has to spend hours researching, then analyze the facts, construct thesis and arguments, and come up with a meaningful conclusion. This takes a great deal of time and energy. By hiring an essay service, you save your resourses for other activities and still get a high quality paper. Sounds like a win-win situation, doesn't it?
