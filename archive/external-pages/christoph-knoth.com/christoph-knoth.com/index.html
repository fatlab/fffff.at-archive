Hi, I am Christoph Knoth and this is my website.

Together with Konrad Renner I share a virtual studio. We work on visual strategies, identities and websites. I also teach with Konrad at the HFBK — the University of Fine Arts of Hamburg in the class Digitale Grafik.

Follow me on Twitter, Instagram or send me an E-Mail
