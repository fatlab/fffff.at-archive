A Jumper for Amy

Found sweater hand-embroidered for Amy Winehouse with all of her tattoos depicted in all the anatomically correct places.


 

I thought about other ways of making this sweater, but decided that it had to be embroidered by hand so that it would be as unique as the distinctive tattoos it covers up, and because embroidery is like a tattoo on fabric.
