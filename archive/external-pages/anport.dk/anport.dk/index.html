What's going on?

• I've released a new album with 16 tracks of my experimental electronica. The album is entitled 'Skip Pipper' and you can listen to it right here on Bandcamp.

• I've helped Mihai Cucos building his own website using WordPress.

• I've finished a comic script with Søren Mosdal. The working title is 'Park World' and 12 pages long. The comic will probably be featured in the forthcoming issue of the Danish magazine 'Fiesta Magasinet' to come out in December 2025. 

• I've deleted my old WordPress site on this server and started to investigate other Content Management Systems. I played around with the open source software Grav and Wonder but ended up with Bludit as it seems to serve all my needs for building a small website in a very easy and uncomplicated way.
