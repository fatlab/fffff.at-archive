I’m Seb Lee-Delisle, a BAFTA winning artist, presenter and coder; I use computers to make large-scale interactive laser light installations and I’m the person behind Liberation laser show software. I also have a YouTube channel, perform at comedy events, and speak at conferences.

This is my personal website, if you’re interested in my larger projects, visit my company website. If you’d like to hire me for your conference or event, please get in touch.
