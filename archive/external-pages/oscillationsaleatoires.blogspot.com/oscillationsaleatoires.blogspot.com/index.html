Moss FM. by Fabienne Felder

Moss FM is the World’s first plant-powered radio by Fabienne Felder in collaboration with Dr. Paolo Bombelli. This is made possible thanks to Photo Microbial Fuel Cells (Photo-MFCs), which harness and convert electrons produced by plants during photosynthesis. Moss tufts are essentially used as biological solar panels in this emerging biophilic technology.
