Friday, April 2 2021

Pulsar​.​scramble vol. 3

3 Presets: 1 Preset, 2 Preset, 3 Preset

The final volume of the Pulsar.scramble series. All tracks are composed using pulsar synthesis, an advanced form of particle synthesis used to generate trains of sonic particles. Its output ranges from singular impulses and sequences to continuous tones across multiple perceptual timescales.

Most of the material presented in this series uses the New Pulsar Generator (nuPg) software designed by Marcin Pietruszewski.

released April 2, 2021
