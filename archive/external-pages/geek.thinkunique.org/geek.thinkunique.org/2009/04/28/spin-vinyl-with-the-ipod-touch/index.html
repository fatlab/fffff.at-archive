28 April 2009 @ 7:49Spin Vinyl with the iPod touch

Theodore Watson created an app for the iPod touch which allows you to spin the touch, and vary the playback speed of the music based on the speed of the spin. Pretty cool!

It’s been stated that this only works with the 2nd gen iPod touch due to its curved back, but I’m sure that some cases might allow you to spin a 1st gen. I don’t doubt that this would work with the iPhone as well.
