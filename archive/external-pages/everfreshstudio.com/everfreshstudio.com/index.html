    ABOUT

    Emerging during the street art boom of the early 2000s, Everfresh Studio has been a cornerstone of Melbourne's street art scene for over two decades. Currently in its 4th location in Collingwood.

    ​

    This unique space serves as a rare cultural hub within Melbourne’s inner suburbs, dedicated to nurturing independent artistic careers, challenging conventional institutional frameworks, and mentoring new generations of street artists, graffiti writers, muralists and more.

    ​

    Everfresh operates as a private studio housing some of Australia’s most renowned contemporary artists and public art practitioners, moving beyond paint on walls to a wide variety of visual arts practices. The studio has been at its Perry street premises for the past 11 years. Now a multidiscipline creative hub of painters, sculptors, screen printers, sign writers, designers, photographers,  installation artists & makers.

    OUR STORY

    Art on the Street

    ​

    Since the rise of street art in Melbourne, one prolific group of artists has continually turned Melbourne’s streets into their canvases. The group is known as Everfresh, and its work is a fusion of explosive colour, intricate line work, iconic characters and visual stories that can be appreciated (or condemned) not only in the streets and laneways of their native Melbourne, but in public spaces around the world. 

    ​

    Everfresh Studio was formed in 2004 in response to the closure of another inner-city set-up, Blender studios. Initially four artists, Rone, Reka, Sync and Wonderlust cleared out an unused storage shed and the first incarnation of Everfresh was created. Soon after Phibs and Meggs joined. This new suburban base allowed the artists to broaden the reaches of their work to include all of Collingwood and the neighbouring suburbs of Fitzroy and Brunswick, as well as the whole of the city’s centre. In time, these areas and the studio members and other artists operating within them would make Melbourne famous for the impressive extent and indisputable quality of the street art and graffiti adorning its walls, and in turn, making the city a kind of mecca for street and graffiti artists and enthusiasts alike., Once moving to the Easey st location, which was the HQ for 7 years, Makatron + Tooth joined. These highly energetic years saw multiple exhibitions, many many murals, and publishing of a book, launched in Tokyo, Sydney and Melbourne. The studio base saw many artists come and go, using it as a crash spot and creative space. Tom Civil joined the studio bringing years of experience.

    ​

    Inside the studio, the place is a visual overload, where chaos and colour meet you at every turn. Wet paint stains your clothes and spilled glue sticks and stencil debris to your shoes. Bicycles hang from the ceiling in varying states of disrepair. Canvases—even precious ones—are wedged and stacked wherever there’s a nook, and once brilliant-red fire extinguishers slowly begin to fossilise next to a shelf of vintage spray cans. 

    ​

    Now in our 11th year at our 4th location at 5 Perry st Collingwood, much of the studio members have changed, yet still keeping the vibrancy and innovative spirit alive that makes Everfresh what it is.

    ART RESIDENCY

    Bringing Artists Together

     

    Everfresh has always been a home away from home for like-minded artists. We ran an Artist Residency for a number of years until Pandemic stopped travel. 

    We plan to start again in 2026. 

     

    We offer a basic bedroom/studio and very basic shower in the middle of Collingwood, and access to our shared indoor + outdoor work spaces.

     

    If you are interested in a short term residency, for a very small fee for a few weeks at a time, please get in touch. The one condition is we need someone to vouch for you, a studio member or someone who is a trusted friend of the studio.
