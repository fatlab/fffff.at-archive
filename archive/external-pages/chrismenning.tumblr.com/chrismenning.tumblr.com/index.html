These Grilled Curry Chicken Skewers are a flavorful and aromatic twist on classic barbecue. The marinade infuses the chicken with rich curry flavors, creating a delicious and tender dish perfect for outdoor grilling.

Ingredients: 1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces. ¼ cup plain yogurt. 2 tablespoons curry powder. 1 tablespoon olive oil. 2 cloves garlic, minced. 1 tablespoon ginger, grated. 1 teaspoon cumin. 1 teaspoon paprika. Salt and pepper to taste. Wooden skewers, soaked in water.

Instructions: In a bowl, mix yogurt, curry powder, olive oil, minced garlic, grated ginger, cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper to create the marinade. Add the chicken pieces to the marinade, ensuring each piece is well-coated. Marinate for at least 1 hour or overnight in the refrigerator. Preheat the grill to medium-high heat. Thread marinated chicken pieces onto soaked wooden skewers. Grill the skewers for 8-10 minutes, turning occasionally, until the chicken is cooked through and has a nice char. Serve hot and enjoy your Grilled Curry Chicken Skewers!

Delicious chocolate and peanut butter cake pops shaped like footballs, perfect for game day or any chocolate-peanut butter lover’s celebration.

Ingredients: 2 cups chocolate cake crumbs. 1 cup creamy peanut butter. ½ cup powdered sugar. 1 cup chocolate chips, melted. 1 tablespoon vegetable oil. White decorating icing.

Instructions: In a large bowl, mix together chocolate cake crumbs, peanut butter, and powdered sugar until well combined. Shape the mixture into small football shapes and place them on a parchment-lined tray. Chill the football-shaped cake pops in the refrigerator for 30 minutes. In a microwave-safe bowl, melt chocolate chips and vegetable oil in 30-second intervals, stirring until smooth. Dip each chilled football cake pop into the melted chocolate, ensuring it’s fully coated. Allow excess chocolate to drip off. Place the chocolate-coated footballs back on the parchment-lined tray and let them set in the refrigerator for another 30 minutes. Using white decorating icing, pipe football stitching onto each cake pop to create a football design. Once the icing has set, the Chocolate Peanut Butter Footballs are ready to be enjoyed!

Elevate your meals with the rich flavors of Queen City Sausage paired with vibrant, grilled vegetables. This recipe brings together the savory goodness of high-quality sausage with the freshness of charred veggies, creating a feast for your taste buds.

Ingredients: 1 package Queen City Sausage your preferred variety. Assorted fresh vegetables bell peppers, onions, zucchini, etc., sliced. Olive oil. Salt and pepper to taste. Your favorite spices and herbs paprika, garlic powder, thyme, etc..

Instructions: Warm up your grill or grill pan on the stove over medium-high heat. Grill the Queen City Sausage until it’s done and has a little char on it. To make sure it cooks evenly, flip it over every so often. Add the sliced vegetables to a large bowl. Add the olive oil, salt, pepper, and any other spices and herbs you like. Season the vegetables and put them on the grill. Cook them until they are soft but still crunchy and have a nice char. Cut the Queen City Sausage into thin slices and put them on top of the grilled vegetables. If you want, you can serve it with mustard or your favorite dipping sauce. Enjoy the bold and flavorful Queen City Sausage with vegetables that have been cooked just right.

Ground chicken mixed with hot sauce and blue cheese makes these Spicy Buffalo Chicken Burgers a tasty take on the classic burger. Great for burger fans who like their food a little spicy.

Ingredients: 1 lb ground chicken. ¼ cup breadcrumbs. ¼ cup hot sauce. ¼ cup crumbled blue cheese. ¼ cup diced celery. ¼ cup diced onion. ½ tsp garlic powder. ½ tsp paprika. Salt and pepper to taste. 4 burger buns. Lettuce leaves and tomato slices for topping. Additional hot sauce for drizzling.

Instructions: Put breadcrumbs, hot sauce, blue cheese crumbles, diced celery and onion, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper in a bowl. Mix the ingredients together. Mix everything together well. Split the mixture in half and shape each half into a burger patty. Set your grill or griddle on the stove to medium-high heat to get it ready. Make sure the chicken burger patties are cooked all the way through and have a nice char on the outside. This should take about 5 to 6 minutes per side. Toast the burger buns a little on the grill or in a toaster while the burgers are cooking. When the burgers are done, put a tomato slice and a lettuce leaf on the bottom half of each bun, then add a chicken patty on top of each one. If you like your food really spicy, add more hot sauce to the chicken patties. Put the top half of the burger bun on top, and serve right away. Take a bite of your Spicy Buffalo Chicken Burgers!

Easy to make pulled pork in a slow cooker that is tender and full of flavor. Great for sandwiches with a tangy BBQ kick.

Ingredients: 3 lbs pork shoulder, trimmed of excess fat. 1 cup BBQ sauce. ½ cup apple cider vinegar. ¼ cup brown sugar. 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce. 1 tablespoon mustard. 1 teaspoon smoked paprika. 1 teaspoon garlic powder. 1 teaspoon onion powder. ½ teaspoon black pepper. ½ teaspoon salt. 8 hamburger buns.

Instructions: Put brown sugar, Worcestershire sauce, mustard, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, black pepper, and black pepper in a bowl. Mix the ingredients together. Put the pork shoulder in the crock pot. Make sure the pork shoulder is evenly covered with the BBQ sauce mixture as you pour it over it. The pork should be soft enough to shred with a fork after 8 to 10 hours on low heat or 4 to 6 hours on high heat. Take the pork out of the slow cooker when it’s done cooking and shred it with two forks. Put the shredded pork back in the slow cooker and add the rest of the sauce. If you want, you can toast the hamburger buns. Buns should be used to serve the pulled pork. Have fun!

This Crock Pot German Meatloaf is a flavorful twist on a classic dish. The slow cooker method ensures a moist and tender meatloaf packed with savory spices and herbs.

Ingredients: 1 ½ lbs ground beef. 1 cup breadcrumbs. 1 onion, finely chopped. 2 eggs. ½ cup milk. ¼ cup ketchup. 2 tablespoons mustard. 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce. 1 teaspoon salt. ½ teaspoon black pepper. ½ teaspoon garlic powder. ½ teaspoon paprika. ¼ teaspoon ground nutmeg. ¼ cup chopped parsley.

Instructions: In a large bowl, mix together ground beef, breadcrumbs, onion, eggs, milk, ketchup, mustard, Worcestershire sauce, salt, black pepper, garlic powder, paprika, nutmeg, and parsley until well combined. Shape the mixture into a loaf and place it in the slow cooker. Cover and cook on low for 6-8 hours or on high for 3-4 hours, or until the meatloaf is cooked through. Serve slices of the meatloaf with your favorite sides and enjoy!

Enjoy the complex tastes of Filipino pork barbecue, which is a tasty mix of sweet, savory, and smoky flavors. Great for grilling and sharing with friends and family.

Ingredients: 2 pounds pork shoulder, thinly sliced into strips. 1 cup soy sauce. 1 cup banana ketchup. 1 cup pineapple juice. 1 cup brown sugar. 1 head garlic, minced. 1 teaspoon ground black pepper. Bamboo skewers, soaked in water.

Instructions: In a bowl, combine soy sauce, banana ketchup, pineapple juice, brown sugar, minced garlic, and ground black pepper to make the marinade. Add the thinly sliced pork strips to the marinade, ensuring each piece is well-coated. Marinate in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours or overnight for best results. Thread the marinated pork strips onto soaked bamboo skewers, dividing them evenly. Preheat the grill or barbecue to medium-high heat. Grill the pork skewers for 8-10 minutes, turning occasionally and basting with the marinade until the pork is fully cooked and has a nice char. Serve the Filipino Pork Barbecue hot off the grill with steamed rice and your favorite dipping sauce. Enjoy this delicious and flavorful dish!

These tasty, vegan-friendly, AIP-compliant Paleo Pumpkin Blondies are a great fall treat. They have no refined sugar or grains and are flavorful, moist, and ideal for sating your sweet tooth.

Ingredients: 1 cup pumpkin puree. ½ cup coconut flour. ¼ cup coconut oil, melted. ¼ cup maple syrup or honey for non-vegan option. ¼ cup coconut milk. 1 tsp vanilla extract. 1 tsp cinnamon. ½ tsp ground ginger. ¼ tsp ground nutmeg. ¼ tsp sea salt. ½ tsp baking soda. Optional: ¼ cup chopped pecans or walnuts.

Instructions: Set the oven temperature to 175C 350F. Use coconut oil to grease or line an 8 x 8-inch baking dish with parchment paper. Pumpkin puree, coconut flour, melted coconut oil, honey or maple syrup, coconut milk, vanilla extract, nutmeg, cinnamon, and sea salt should all be combined in a big mixing bowl. Blend until completely smooth. Stir the baking soda into the mixture until it is well combined. If using, fold in the chopped nuts. Evenly distribute the batter after pouring it into the baking dish that has been ready. A toothpick inserted into the center should come out clean after baking for 25 to 30 minutes in a preheated oven, or until the edges are golden brown. Take out of the oven and allow it cool fully before slicing it into square pieces. Present and savor!

A delicious skillet dinner made with Italian sausage, zucchini, red bell pepper, and brown rice cooked all in one pan.

Ingredients: 1 lb Italian sausage. 1 medium zucchini, sliced. 1 red bell pepper, diced. 1 cup brown rice, uncooked. 2 cups chicken broth. 1 tsp Italian seasoning. Salt and pepper to taste. ½ cup shredded mozzarella cheese. Fresh parsley for garnish optional.

Instructions: In a large skillet, cook Italian sausage over medium heat until browned and cooked through. Drain excess grease. Add sliced zucchini and diced red bell pepper to the skillet. Cook until vegetables are tender, about 5-7 minutes. Stir in uncooked brown rice, chicken broth, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Bring mixture to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until rice is cooked and most of the liquid is absorbed. Sprinkle shredded mozzarella cheese over the top and cover until cheese is melted. Garnish with fresh parsley if desired. Serve hot and enjoy!

You can make these vegan oat bars in no time at all for a quick breakfast or snack. They taste great and are full of good things for you, so the whole family loves them!

Ingredients: 2 cups rolled oats. 1 cup almond butter. ½ cup maple syrup. ½ cup chopped almonds. ½ cup dried cranberries. ¼ cup ground flaxseed. ¼ cup hemp seeds. 1 teaspoon vanilla extract. ½ teaspoon cinnamon. ¼ teaspoon salt.

Instructions: Warm the oven up to 350F 175C and put parchment paper in a baking dish. Put the rolled oats, almond butter, maple syrup, almonds, cranberries, flaxseed, hemp seeds, vanilla extract, cinnamon, and salt in a large bowl. Mix the ingredients together until they are well mixed. Firmly press the mixture into the baking dish that has been prepared. Put it in the oven for 20 to 25 minutes, or until the edges turn golden brown. Let it cool down all the way before cutting it into bars.
