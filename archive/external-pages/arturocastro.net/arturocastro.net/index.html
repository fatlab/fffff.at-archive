Arturo Castro is an artist, educator and engineer currently based in Berlin. He is one of the core developers of the open source toolkit for artists, designers and researchers openFrameworks. Currently he works on his own and collaborates with other artists and technologists on projects usually in the field of interactive and generative installations.

Part of his practice consist in developing creative tools that enable and facilitate other artists and designers work with technology.

His work has been exhibited in museums like Maison d'Ailleurs in Switzerland, the London Design Museum, Eyebeam or Moscow's Multimedia Art Museum among others.
