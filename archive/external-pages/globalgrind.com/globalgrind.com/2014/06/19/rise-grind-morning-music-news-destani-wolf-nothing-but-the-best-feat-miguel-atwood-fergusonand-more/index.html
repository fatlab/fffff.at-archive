RISE & GRIND! Morning Music News: Destani Wolf “Nothing But The Best” (Feat. Miguel Atwood Ferguson)…AND MORE!

Written by martinblondet
Published on June 19, 2014

Share the post

Share this link via

Or copy link

L.A.-based singer/songwriter Destani Wolf has released a new single entitled “Nothing But The Best” featuring Miguel Atwood-Ferguson, in anticipation of her forthcoming EP Peek Away. Put a little soul in your morning with this ’70s funk-tinged shot of Latin jazziness. Listen to the track above. [SoundCloud]

Common has released a Hype Williams-directed music video for his song “Kingdom” featuring Vince Staples. The single will be on his upcoming album Nobody’s Smiling, dropping July 22 via Def Jam Records. The cinematic video features neither rapper, but highlights several issues affecting the troubled town of Chicago through striking imagery. Check out the video here.

Oh boy…Businessman Dame Dash and Dipset rapper Cam’ron have announced they will be opening a Mediterranean restaurant called Dish. The restaurant will be opening soon in Dumbo, Brooklyn. Will Dame Dash and Killa Cam’s entrepreneurial spirit prevail in the restaurant business? Only a taste will tell. [Vibe]

After the massive success of her Iggy Azalea-assisted single “Problem,” Arianna Grande‘s next single is rumored to be entitled “Break Free” and is produced by Zedd. The single will be on her upcoming as- yet-untitled album, which is due for release in the fall. The singer also previewed some of the imagery featured on the new single’s music video on her Instagram. [PopCrush]

The best tech update you could possibly make today would have to be adding these Kanye West-head icons to your dock. Created by F.A.T. Studios, the process simply replaces each icon in your dock with one of the many faces of Yeezy. Add an element of greatness to your laptop by following these steps. [Complex]
