LindsayHoward.Net is now part of WastedTalentInc.com

LindsayHoward.Net was the personal website of Lindsay Howard, a talented and accomplished artist and designer. The website served as a virtual portfolio showcasing Lindsay’s creative works, which span various mediums such as painting, sculpture, digital art, and installations. In 2023, the LindsayHoward.Net website was purchased and became part of the WastedTalentInc.com group of art-related websites that also includes ArtSoiree.com and FitzroyGallery.com. Below are just some articles that complement the readers of all my websites. My focus will be to provide artists and followers of Lindsay Howard with a space to expand on their learning of art and a space to promote their artworks and for artists to list current and future physical exhibition spaces and shows.
