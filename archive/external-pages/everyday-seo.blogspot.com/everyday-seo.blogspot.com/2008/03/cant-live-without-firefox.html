Cant Live without FireFox

Posted by yahrah Saturday, March 29, 2008 at 1:20 PM

Ohh Yeah! thats right..I have tried IE, Safari, Netscape and other browsers, But FireFox is made to be compatible on doing SEO. Almost all kind of tools for SEO was made available by Firefox addons and theres also addons made for social bookmarking sites. Heres the quicklist of addons from FireFox i use for SEO.

1. Search Status - Display the Google PageRank, Alexa rank and Compete ranking anywhere in your browser, along with fast keyword density analyser, keyword/nofollow highlighting, backward/related links, Alexa info and more.

2. SEOpen - Provides some basic tools to help with search engine optimization. Including google backlinks, yahoo backlinks, PageRank check, http header viewer, and more. All features are available by right-clicking on an open area of a web page, or by using...

3. Copy Links - Copy multiple URLs in anchors to clipboard...

4. SEOHAND - SEO services: Track sites placement at Google and Yahoo for any keyword. Watch Google PageRank (PR), Backlinks, Saturation over time. Graph and table presentation of the data.
