Buy an Advent Calendar

October 28, 2024Eliotadvent calendar, board game, calendar, christmas, exit Leave a comment

I have an alert on November 1st every year to buy an advent calendar. Years ago I made my own liquor advent calendar, but last year I got this excellent Exit The Game advent calendar. Exit is a series of escape room style puzzle games you can find sold alongside hobby board games. For the advent calendar, it becomes a daily series of puzzles. Each day the puzzle solution will lead to the next door to open; they’re not numbered sequentially like a regular calendar. I don’t want to give up too much but the inventiveness of the puzzles and the variety day to day was just incredible. More than once I walked people through the days I had opened to show off the mechanics. Even looking at the cover now, that empty white circle spot light seems out of place and will probably be used in a puzzle. This was one of my favorite gaming experiences from last year. I highly recommend you pick EXIT: The Game – Advent Calendar for this year.

Addendum: I was prompted to write this post after seeing this list of nerdy advent calendars. Maybe you’ll find something fun in there too. I’ve bought the Lego advent calendars in the past for other people, not wanting to fill my own house with little bits of Lego. Keep your eyes peeled though, if you see ads for advent calendars on social media, there is a high chance it is a scam. A discerning eye will be able to spot inconsistencies in the scale and detail of the calendar items illustrated. Stick to known retailers when buying. Happy holidays!
